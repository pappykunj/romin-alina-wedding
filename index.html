Sacred Symbols & Rituals

1

The Minnu (Thali)

Sacred leaf-shaped gold pendant tied around the bride's neck with seven threads taken from the Manthrakodi saree.

2

The Manthrakodi

Rich silk wedding saree gifted by the groom and blessed by the priest, draped over the bride's head during vows.

3

Solemn Vows & Rings

The exchanging of holy wedding vows and rings before God, family, and loved ones during the church mass.
